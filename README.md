
🚗 EV Adoption Behavior & Range Anxiety Analysis
📊 Dataset Description

This project uses a dataset containing customer demographic, behavioral, environmental, commuting, and EV-related information. Key variables include age, annual income, daily commute distance, city type, environmental concern, range anxiety, charging availability, subsidies, and EV purchase intention.

🎯 Project Overview

An exploratory data analysis project investigating the factors associated with EV purchase intention and range anxiety using Python.

🔍 Objectives
Analyze customer characteristics and EV adoption behavior.
Examine the relationship between range anxiety and EV purchase intention.
Study the influence of charging availability, subsidies, environmental concern, income, commuting distance, and city type.
Identify meaningful patterns and generate business-oriented insights.
🛠️ Tools & Techniques
Python
Pandas & NumPy
Matplotlib & Seaborn
Data cleaning and preprocessing
groupby(), aggregation and percentage analysis
Univariate, bivariate & multivariate analysis
Correlation analysis
📈 EDA & Visualizations
Performed univariate, bivariate, and multivariate EDA using statistical summaries, groupby(), and aggregations.
Analyzed relationships between EV purchase intention, range anxiety, charging infrastructure, subsidies, environmental concern, city type, income, and commuting behavior.
Created 10+ visualizations, including histograms, bar/count plots, box plots, scatter plots, pair plots, and a correlation heatmap.
Used visualizations to identify patterns and support the findings from the EDA.
💡 Key Insights
Rural customers showed the highest EV purchase intention percentage (18.98%), followed by suburban (17.68%) and urban (16.92%) customers.
Charging availability showed an association with EV purchase intention and was explored as a potential contributing factor.
Range anxiety was examined as an important factor associated with EV adoption.
Subsidy availability and environmental concern were also associated with EV purchase intention.
Commuting distance and charging infrastructure provided additional context for understanding range anxiety.
✅ Conclusion

The analysis shows that EV adoption is influenced by a combination of customer characteristics, range anxiety, charging infrastructure, environmental concerns, financial incentives, and commuting behavior. The findings can help EV manufacturers, charging providers, and policymakers understand potential barriers and opportunities for wider EV adoption.
